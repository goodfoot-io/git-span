# Recommended beachhead

The strongest initial customer profile is:

> **An API, infrastructure, or developer-tool company with a polyglot repository, multiple manually maintained SDKs or distributions, active coding-agent adoption, and recurring review comments about forgotten companion changes.**

This segment has several advantages:

- the failure mode is easy to explain;
- the homepage example maps directly to the customer's work;
- the relationships are often stable enough to curate;
- omissions are measurable;
- the repository can often be handled without cross-repository support;
- the buyer is identifiable: Developer Platform, DevEx, API Platform, or AI Enablement;
- the customer does not require the compliance features of a regulated enterprise.

The recommended initial positioning is:

> **Git-span prevents partial changes. It gives coding agents and reviewers the nonlocal obligations attached to the exact code they touch, then detects when those obligations have been left unresolved.**

Leading with "implicit dependencies" alone is not recommended. That describes the underlying technical object but not the purchased outcome. The purchased outcome is **complete change**.
